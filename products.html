Our Product Range

We Offer a Range of Safety Solutions to Meet Your Industrial Needs

Protective wear Products

Raxwell provides premium protective clothing designed to safeguard workers in high-risk environments. From certified microporous coveralls and heavy-duty chemical suits to high-visibility vests and chest waders, our protective wear combines max comfort with ultimate industrial compliance.

Hand Protection Products

Engineered for maximum dexterity, durability, and protection against mechanical, thermal, electrical, and chemical hazards. Our comprehensive hand safety line offers solutions for heavy construction, electrical maintenance, precision work, and chemical handling.

Respiratory & Face Protection

Ensure clean airflow and facial protection with Raxwell’s advanced respiratory systems and face shields. Protect your workforce against organic vapors, chemical fumes, hazardous dust particles, and impact splashes with certified, ergonomic equipment.

Head & Body Safety Products

Reliable head-to-toe protection engineered to absorb heavy impacts and meet international industrial safety standards. From vented hard hats equipped with customizable suspension systems to light-duty bump caps, we ensure maximum worker comfort and safety.

Traffic, Road Safety & Site Control

Maximize site visibility and manage traffic flows effectively with solar-powered warning systems, heavy-duty rubber cones, and durable traffic control equipment engineered for extreme weather conditions.

Facility, LOTO & Emergency Equipment

Protect personnel during maintenance and maintain facility readiness with high-grade Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) safety devices, emergency eyewash stations, and outdoor safety accessories designed to safeguard high-risk workplaces.
